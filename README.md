# Project-DX2 Is Built On A Program I Developed Called VTech MAXIMIZE
**(Created by JustChill6)**

**vtech MAXIMIZE** is the **first ready-to-use, plug-and-play mod and management suite on planet Earth** built for the **VTech KidiZoom Smartwatch DX2** — including newer, heavily locked UK/EU `1938C` (`54-193800-A03-000-S00`) units where on-device factory test menus and early v1.0 firmware exploits are completely blocked.

While previous community attempts stalled at experimental bootloader notes requiring early v1.0 hardware or disassembly, **vtech MAXIMIZE** works cleanly over standard Micro-USB with zero brick risk.

---

## Key Features
* **1-Click Custom Gallery Logo Installer:** Automatically detects your watch's `DCIM\<NNN>VTECH` directory prefix, renames your mod image to match (`<NNN>_0001.JPG`), and converts it to a native **`240×240` Baseline JPEG** — completely eliminating the watch's dreaded `File Error` screen.
* **Automatic Photo Clock Face (`PCF_0001.BIN`) Injection:** Automatically installs pre-configured Photo Clock Face binaries directly into `VTE_DATA\LL_SAVE\54-193800-A03-000-S00\` so your custom watch face persists across reboots.
* **Full Watch Backup & Restore:** Creates complete backups of your `DCIM`, `VOICE`, and `VTE_DATA` save partitions in seconds and restores them anytime.
* **Live `[DEVLOG]` & Native Windows GUI Pop-Ups:** Tracks every step in real time and alerts you with native `Download Finished!` or `Download Failed, Check DevLog.` dialogs.

---

## How to Use
1. Download and extract the entire `MOD` folder into your Windows **Downloads** folder.
2. Make sure the **Master Power Switch** on the back of your VTech DX2 is switched **ON**, then plug the watch into your PC via a **Micro-USB data cable**.
3. Double-click **`vtech_MAXIMIZE.exe`**, press **`1`** to install the full mod, and wait for the **`Download Finished!`** pop-up before unplugging!
